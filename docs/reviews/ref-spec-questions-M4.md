## Readings adopted by the reference without a question — REF-M4 (all confirmed by the reviewer, Weisung REF-M4-1)

The reviewer's wording: "readings 1–15 are all confirmed", including 7 (the §4.1(a) decoder rejects a low-order `ek_I`/`spk_dh`/`opk_dh`/`ik_dh` before any DH; the §6.4 non-zero check stays as defence in depth and needs no vector), 8, 12 and 13. The restructured positive run and the added V3, V8, R11 and R12 are accepted as the case table. Items 1–10 concern the protocol, items 11–15 the vector file.

1. **Transcript integers (§6.4).** `spk_id` and `opk_id` are u32 big-endian; `encode(IKSPublic_*)` is the 2017-byte encoding; the encapsulation keys are hashed as encoded. I takes `IKSPublic_R` and the bundle fields from the decoded LinkDataV1, R from its own keys; both give the same bytes.
2. **Bundle signature (§6.3).** The signed message is the encoded bundle fields before `sig`, followed by the signer's `ik_dh` (4434 B, SCHEMA §4.5 row 7). V7 signs the fields with `opk_present` = 0 and the OPK bytes kept.
3. **Invitee (§5.5): order and checks.** Step 1 is the URI, the InvitationV1 decoder and `expires` > `now` (expired iff `now` ≥ `expires`; no case on the boundary). Then CAEAD.Open with `K_ld` and the LinkDataV1 decoder (padding, §4.1 obligations, `opk_present` = 1). Step 3 compares the fingerprint in constant time. Step 4 runs HybridVerify, then `spk_expiry` > `now`. Neither of the inviter's bounds of SQ-25 is checked. Every invitee rejection is the one uniform error.
4. **The URI (§5.2).** Exact prefix `secmp://i/`, then canonical base64url without padding; anything else is malformed. No case depends on this strictness; `ref/tests/test_hx.py` tests it.
5. **Responder grouping (§6.5, §6.6 step 1).** Cells are trial-opened in fetch order; a cell that does not open, is not a valid HandshakeCellPlaintext, or repeats an (`init_id`, `i`) already seen is ignored (first seen wins). The first `init_id` with chunks 0, 1 and 2 is processed, one group per `respond` call. "No complete group" is the op's uniform rejection (R6, R11, R12).
6. **Responder step 1.** `spk_id` must equal the recorded `spk_id` and `opk_id` the recorded `opk_id` (R1, R2); then the OPK must still be unused (R3).
7. **Checks at decode on R's side.** Outer and Inner go through the M2 decoders, so the §4.1 decoder obligations apply; R4's `ek_I` = 0 is rejected by the Outer decoder before any DH; IKSPublic_I gets every IKSPublic check.
8. **The first message.** After Decrypt, R decodes the Content (padding, `caps` = 0) and requires type 0x01; it checks neither `seq` nor `ts`, nor the header's `pn`/`n`.
9. **No time at R.** §6.6 steps 1–4 take no `now`.
10. **What R keeps.** A rejection changes none of R's state; success deletes only the OPK. Retiring the invitation queue and record is outside the suite.
11. **R8 and R9 list R's DH-step draws** (`dh_sk`, `kem_seed`, `m`) after the construction draws, like tr's N1.
12. **R9** encrypts from I's post-init TR state, so its `first_msg` uses the message key of case 6's (n = 0) for another plaintext. Only a vector may do that.
13. **The two `onion`s.** The invitation's RelayRef takes `onion_pubkey` (32 raw stream bytes) as PUBKEY; the Handshake route's RelayRef takes the Ed25519 public key of `onion_seed`.
14. **V8** reads "the `K_ld` of `link_key` with bit 0 flipped" as `flip(link_key, 0)` (byte 0 XOR 0x01, SCHEMA §4).
15. **Vector-file shape** (SCHEMA §1 `"schema": 5`, SCHEMA-4.10). `uri` is an ASCII string, `accept` a boolean, `opks_post` an ascending array of numbers, `fetched` and `routes` arrays of byte strings; `content` is unpadded; an `invitee-reject` case has no `outputs`.
