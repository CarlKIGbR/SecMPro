## 5. caead_derive SUB_FLOOR_SHIFT

These are hand checks of the F16 rule (`possible_verdicts`, ctreport.rs:633-676).
- **Shift reproduced.** |t| > 4.5 in both measurements with the same sign at p50, p75, p90, p95 and p99.
- **Deciding crop.** It is the strongest min(|t1|, |t2|): p95 = 45.45, against p90 39.24, p75 39.98, p99 20.46 and p50 19.19. The recorded crop p95 matches.
- **Floor.** Floor = max(24.5 ± 0.0005, 24.454) = 24.5 ticks. Largest |Δ| over all crops: 2.553 / 2.550 ticks at p99 = 0.104 floors.
  - floor_surely (|Δ| − 5e-5 ≥ 24.5005) is false everywhere, so fail_sure = false.
  - floor_maybe is false everywhere too, so FAIL is not even possible.
  - sub_floor_possible = true. The set of possible verdicts is {SUB_FLOOR_SHIFT}, and the recorded verdict is admissible.
- **Size.** Per call (k = 3) Δ ≈ 0.81 ticks ≈ 0.33 ns on a median of 3454 ticks (≈ 1.41 µs per batch), a relative shift of about 7·10⁻⁴.
- **Distance to FAIL.** Δ would have to grow by a factor of about 9.6, roughly +22 ticks ≈ 9 ns per batch.
